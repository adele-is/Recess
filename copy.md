# Panel 2 — Just me

## Door card

**Tag:** Just me

**Heading:** One woman. For when you need the space to do something.

## Panel

**Photo:** adele.jpeg

**Photo alt text:** Adele Cubitt Cohen

**Title:**

Me. For the moments when you need it.
**And for more life when you don't.**

**Body:**

We all need a little something. I'm the less obvious kind — but maybe the obvious
is what's got us here.

Send me the doc. Talk me through the thing. Let me find the point — or light the
spark so you do. Or just a natter, because sometimes a perfectly timed
conversation and a laugh is all you need to get back to brilliant.

Like a work wife. But at this stage of your career, when that's harder to find.

You be you. Not the version that shows up at work. The one who doesn't know if
that thing is good, or has been handed a dog's breakfast with two hours to turn
it around, or whose spider senses are firing and doesn't know whether to go red
or blue pill.

I'll help. I'll turn it around. I'll call bullshit — theirs too. And ignite your
spark. Because there is nothing I love more than helping people think, finding
the point, and a bit of well-placed disruption.

Sharp thinker, high EQ. Human rights, humanitarian protection, philanthropy
strategies, social impact and health solutions. Generalist by design. Sharp and
witty by nature.

**Offerings:**

- **The Call** — The brilliant friend who happens to know things. Send me to the
  meeting. Talk me through it. Fast, sharp, no faff.
- **The Retainer** — Outside-in perspective, on tap. The inspo, the cut-through,
  the nudge when you're avoiding. Someone who'll say what nobody else will. Or
  come with you and be your eyes and ears.
- **The Work** — When the problem is real and needs doing properly. Strategy,
  advisory, partnerships, corporate affairs. I find the way forward — and ask the
  people nobody else thought to ask.
