The Room

Not a network. Not a
programme. Recess.

Remember when play was easy? You could kick a ball around with strangers, talk rubbish, follow the energy — and somehow the best things happened.

Then we became adults and started adulting too much.

Same circles. Same sectors. Same conversations. Same sensible routines. Podcasts, wellness hacks, LinkedIn hot takes and Instagram inspiration trying to replace the thing we actually need: other humans.

Recess brings that back. A curated room of sharp, witty, interesting people — deliberately mixed across sectors, roles and worlds. Monthly. Closed door. No agenda. The conversation goes where it goes.

Because the magic is not in the content. It is in the people. The laugh. The idea. The side conversation. The unexpected "oh my god, yes". The moment that makes Tuesday feel more alive.

You leave feeling more like yourself than when you arrived. Ready to go back in — possibly with a new idea, a problem solved, or a little more mischief in your system.
